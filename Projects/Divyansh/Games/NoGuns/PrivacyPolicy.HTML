Information Collection and Use

For a better experience, while using our Service, I may require you to provide us with certain information, including but not limited to User’s age, User’s gameplay activities (such as number of times game is played and what buttons are clicked by the user), It contains surveys that collects user opinions for improvement of the app, It contains in app purchases, It contains Ads provided by Admob and Unity Ads. Some of the information mentioned above is collected automatically by the app. Apart from the service providers listed below, I do not sell or share this information with any third party.

The app also collects the following information to run its online features:

  • Player account: when you open the app, it signs you in with Unity Authentication, which gives you an anonymous player ID. You can choose to link your Google account so your progress is kept when you change phones. The Google sign-in is handled by Google and Unity; my servers only receive your Unity player ID, not your Google password.
  • Player name: the unique name you choose, when it was created or last changed, and the IP address it was created from (used only to stop people from registering large numbers of names). After a name change, your old name stays reserved for you for 30 days. Names of players who never finish a match are released after 30 days without opening the game.
  • Game progress: your XP, kills, deaths, matches played and won. It is stored with Unity Cloud Save and on my server, and the game servers confirm your match results so progress can't be faked.
  • Friends: your friend list, friend requests and the players you have blocked. Your friends can see your name, your rank, whether you are online, and which server and room you are playing in, so they can join you.
  • Friend chat: messages you send to your friends (typed text or quick chat messages) are stored on my server so they can be delivered when your friend is offline. A conversation keeps its last 100 messages for up to 30 days, and is deleted when you or your friend removes the other as a friend or blocks them.
  • Room and match chat: messages in a room's lobby and during a match are passed to the other players in that room by the game servers and are not stored after the room closes, unless they are part of a report.
  • Reports: when you report a chat message or a player, I keep the report, the reported message as it was written, and the last chat lines of the room as you saw them, so I can review it and take action against players who break the rules.
  • Technical information: your IP address is used to connect you to the game servers, to pick the server with the lowest ping, and to limit abuse (too many requests from one address).

Chat Safety

Friend chat is only possible between players who have both accepted each other as friends. Messages are filtered automatically for offensive words, but no filter is perfect, so you may still see content that you find inappropriate. You can report any message or player, and block any player: a blocked player can no longer send you friend requests or messages. Please never share personal information such as your real name, address, phone number or passwords in chat. Players who abuse chat may have their messages removed, their name released or their access to online features taken away.
